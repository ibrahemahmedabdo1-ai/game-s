# Changelog
Each game is versioned independently (`version:` in its pubspec; tag `<game>-vX.Y.Z`).

## 1.0.0 (unreleased) - all six apps
- color_reactor: 100 levels, endless, daily, hints, locked + limited-capacity pipes, switch/gates, moving ice hazards, wrong-route penalty
- gravity_switch: 100 levels with checkpoints, rotating platforms, key/gate puzzles, endless survival, daily, 6 skins (Flame)
- shape_eater: 100 stages, endless, daily, combos/chains, power-ups, 5 appearances (Flame)
- perfect_drop: 100 physics levels, endless, daily, bumpers, multiplier bins, 5 skins (Flame)
- color_merge_lab: 100 stages with generated boards, 4 endless layouts, daily, wild/bomb pieces, limited undo
- mini_puzzle_arcade: hub for the five games
- shared: storage, EN/AR localization, AdMob + UMP, audio (new synthesized SFX + music), UI kit, daily missions, Flame surface
