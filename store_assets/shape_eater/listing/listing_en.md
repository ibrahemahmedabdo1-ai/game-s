**Title (max 30):** Shape Eater: Combo Arcade

Drag your shape around the arena and eat objects that match it. Every few meals your shape changes and so do the rules.

- Daily missions with coin rewards
- Combo multipliers and chain reactions
- Shield, slow-time and magnet power-ups
- Moving obstacles that raise the challenge
- Five unlockable appearances and achievements
